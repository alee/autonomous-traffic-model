# Research questions
Version: 0.1 | Date: 2026-11-17 | Status: draft, unreviewed | Authority: OMFA | Author: Codex with user-supplied intent

User intent: explore optimal citywide traffic coordination among autonomous vehicle agents despite independent operation, faults, and bad faith. Purpose: exploratory research, not operational control.

- RQ1: What tradeoffs between safety outcomes, person travel time, reliability, throughput, and fair access emerge across coordination regimes?
- RQ2: How does performance change with participation, incompatible operator policies, communications failures, and bounded adversarial behavior?
- RQ3: When do local efficiency gains produce network spillback, rerouting oscillations, or neighborhood burden?
- RQ4: Which policies maintain acceptable outcomes and recover across scenarios, and what is their cost under benign conditions?
- RQ5: How far do feasible policies fall from an ideal-information reference under identical physical constraints?

No universal optimum is presumed. Identify nondominated policies conditional on scenario, information, physical constraints, and objective definitions. Report unsafe and infeasible outcomes explicitly; normative acceptability thresholds remain unresolved.
