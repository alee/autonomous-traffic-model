# Stakeholder register
Version: 0.1 | Date: 2026-11-17 | Status: draft, unreviewed | Authority: OMFA | Author: Codex with user-supplied intent

## Identified Stakeholders
Passengers and private drivers: travel and access; AV operators: interoperability and incentives; transit agencies and riders: person throughput and reliability; freight and curb users: deliveries and access; city/regional traffic agencies: coordination authority; emergency services: access and recovery; pedestrians, cyclists, disabled travelers, and neighborhood residents: safety, crossings, accessibility, and diverted traffic. Formal power is likely uneven; it has not been elicited.

## Participation Process
Only the initiating user has contributed; no agency, operator, or affected-community consultation has occurred. Proposed process: consult transport specialists on mechanisms, then affected travelers and neighborhood/accessibility representatives on outcomes and unacceptable burdens. This is a proposed consultation process, not co-production achieved.

## Contributions to Model Design
User selected citywide scope and open tradeoffs, and requested independent, faulty, and bad-faith behavior. Other choices are Codex proposals.

## Unresolved Disagreements
No stakeholder disagreements have been elicited. Potential contested choices include private-vehicle versus person throughput, neighborhood diversion, access priority, central authority, and required connectivity. No consensus is assumed.

## Governance and Transparency
Modeling team retains scientific judgments; affected groups should influence value choices and interpretation. Record each substantive contribution, its source, accepted/rejected status, rationale, and review opportunity in decision-log.md. No external messages or recruitment are authorized by this register.
